ABOUT THE INSTITUTE

Education, practical training and academic guidance

Rabindra Institute for Health Training (R.I.H.T.), Centre–67 (IRMA), is a healthcare and skill-based educational institute located at Kandi Madhunia Bypass Road, Kandi, Murshidabad, West Bengal. Guided by the motto “Health for All,” the institute is committed to providing quality healthcare education, paramedical training, open schooling support, and career-oriented skill development programs for students and aspiring healthcare workers.

R.I.H.T. offers various healthcare, paramedical, pharmacy, open schooling, and skill-based courses through its affiliations and associations with reputed organizations such as Indian Rural Medical Association (IRMA), Indian Institute for Health Training (IIHT), Sikkim Skill University (SSU), Bharat Sevak Samaj (BSS), BOSSE, NIOS, WBSCTVESD, World Skill Council (WSC), and World Rural Medical Association (WRMA).

With experienced faculty members, structured academic guidance, practical training support, and a student-friendly learning environment, Rabindra Institute for Health Training aims to develop skilled, responsible, and service-oriented healthcare professionals who can contribute effectively to society, especially in rural and community healthcare sectors.

01

Our Mission

Our mission at Rabindra Institute for Health Training (R.I.H.T.) is to provide quality healthcare education, practical skill-based training, and career-oriented academic support to students from rural and urban communities. We aim to develop competent, responsible, and service-minded healthcare professionals who can contribute to society with knowledge, discipline, and dedication.

We are committed to promoting accessible healthcare education, strengthening primary healthcare awareness, and empowering students through professional training, modern learning methods, and continuous academic guidance.

02

Our Vision

The vision of Rabindra Institute for Health Training (R.I.H.T.) is to promote quality healthcare education and skill-based training for students who wish to build a career in the healthcare sector.

Guided by the motto “Health for All,” the institute aims to prepare skilled, disciplined, and responsible healthcare workers who can serve society and support community healthcare needs.
